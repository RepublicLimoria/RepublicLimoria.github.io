HEAD OF STATE

The Presidency of Limoria

A fictional civic office representing unity, continuity and the constitutional character of the republic.

A SYMBOL OF UNITY

Leadership with a public purpose.

The President of Limoria is imagined as a non-partisan head of state who protects the constitution, represents the republic and brings people together during moments of national importance.

The office is designed around service, transparency and respect for the institutions that make democratic life possible.

THE CIVIC ROLE

What the office represents

01 • UNITY

National representation

Speaks for the shared identity of Limoria while respecting every region, community and tradition.

02 • CONSTITUTION

Institutional guardian

Upholds the fictional constitutional order and protects the balance between public institutions.

03 • SERVICE

Public trust

Encourages ethical leadership, open government and a long-term view of national progress.
