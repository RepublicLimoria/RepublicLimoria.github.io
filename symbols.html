Symbols of Limoria

The visual identity of the fictional republic.

The National Flag

The fictional flag uses three broad fields and a central emblem.

  • Green: nature and renewal.
  • White: peace and equality.
  • Navy: stability and the sea.
  • Gold: hope, achievement and aspiration.

Motto Unity • Progress • Prosperity

National Emblem

A fictional mountain-and-sun emblem represents resilience, nature and a shared future.

National Flower

The fictional Golden Luma is a symbolic flower associated with renewal.

National Animal
