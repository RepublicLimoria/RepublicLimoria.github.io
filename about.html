ABOUT THE NATION

Welcome to Limoria

A fictional republic designed for creative worldbuilding.

Our Story

Limoria is an imagined country whose national identity is built around unity, peaceful cooperation, education, sustainable development and respect for nature. Its cities combine modern architecture with green public spaces, while coastal and highland regions preserve distinctive local traditions.

National Vision

The fictional national vision is simple: build a society where institutions serve people, innovation supports everyday life, and economic development does not come at the expense of future generations.

Identity

The name Limoria is an original fictional name for this project. The national motto is Unity • Progress • Prosperity. The flag uses green, white, navy and gold to represent nature, peace, stability and aspiration.
