Constitution of Limoria

A fictional constitutional framework for the worldbuilding project.

Chapter I — Fundamental Principles

Limoria is founded on human dignity, peaceful cooperation, equality before the law, accountable government and responsible stewardship of nature.

Chapter II — Rights & Freedoms

Fictional citizens are protected by principles of expression, peaceful assembly, education, equal protection and due process within the fictional legal system.

Chapter III — Institutions of Government

Public institutions exercise powers within constitutional limits. The legislature makes laws, the executive administers them and the judiciary interprets them.

Chapter IV — Independent Judiciary

Courts operate independently within the fictional system and may review public actions for constitutional compliance.

Chapter V — Economy & Environment

The fictional state promotes innovation, fair opportunity, sustainable development and protection of natural resources.

Chapter VI — Amendment

Constitutional amendments require broad legislative support and a transparent public process within the fictional republic.
