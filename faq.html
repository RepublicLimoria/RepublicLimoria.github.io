No. Limoria is fictional and exists only as a creative worldbuilding project.

No. All statistics shown on this site are invented for the fictional setting.

Can I apply for Limorian citizenship?

No real application exists. The citizenship page describes a fictional concept only.

No. LIM is a fictional currency created for this project.

Can I use this website for a real government service?

No. It is not a real government website and does not provide legal, immigration, financial or civic services.

Can I customize the country?

Yes. You can edit the HTML content, images and fictional facts to develop your own Limoria universe.
